FAQ

Who are you?

I’m Jose Paredes, a data scientist and AI engineer in Madrid. I build computer-vision systems, automation and local-first products.

What do you write about?

Data, computer vision, automation and local-first AI products: how they work, the trade-offs behind them and the parts that were hard to get right.

Can I use the code?

Yes. The snippets are there to be reused; if a post links to a repository, check that repository’s licence first. Attribution is appreciated but not required.

How can I contact you?

Email me at jparedescolmenarejo@gmail.com , or find me on GitHub and LinkedIn .

Why are some posts in Spanish?

English is the default, but a few topics are easier to explain in Spanish, and the Spanish version helps readers who arrive through Spanish-speaking communities.
