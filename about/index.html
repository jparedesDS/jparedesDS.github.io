About

I am a data scientist and AI engineer based in Madrid. At EIPSA I build computer vision systems and data automation for industrial engineering, from inspection models on the production line to the tooling that surrounds them. Outside of work I build local-first AI products: tools that run on your own machine and keep your data there.

Projects

  • Subany — live subtitles in the browser. Whisper and ONNX on WebGPU, with no server round-trip.
  • CleanYourMail — a local-first way to clean up a Gmail inbox without handing over your mail.
  • CleanYourPhone — the same idea for your phone: inspect and clean it locally.
